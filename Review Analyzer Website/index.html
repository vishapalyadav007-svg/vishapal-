How do I share the website through WhatsApp?

Copy your public HTTPS link, paste it in any WhatsApp chat, and share demo password 21062006. When the recipient opens the link, the Owner Login screen unlocks upon entering the password.

How is the sentiment score calculated?

Our NLP engine scans reviews for positive and negative restaurant keywords, maps mentions to Food, Service, Ambiance, and Value, and calculates an overall percentage and 1-5 star score.
